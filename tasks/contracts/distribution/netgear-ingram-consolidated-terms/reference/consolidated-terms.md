# Consolidated Terms — NETGEAR / Ingram Micro Distributor Agreement, as Amended

This summarizes the Distributor Agreement between **NETGEAR, Inc.** (supplier, "NETGEAR") and **Ingram Micro** (distributor, "Distributor"), as it stands after both amendments. Text in brackets marked `[*]` was redacted from the public SEC filing under a confidential treatment request; this summary does not guess at redacted content.

## Parties

**NETGEAR, Inc.** is the supplier of hardware and software products under the Agreement. **Ingram Micro** is the Distributor.

## Dates

- The base Distributor Agreement has an **Effective Date of March 1, 1996** (NETGEAR Agreement Number N115).
- The **first Amendment** is effective **October 1, 1996** ("Amendment Date").
- **Amendment #2** was entered into on **July 15, 1998**.

## Term and renewal

The Agreement runs for **one year from the Effective Date** and **automatically renews for successive one-year periods** unless terminated as described below. Having first renewed in 1997, the Agreement is currently in a renewal term.

## Termination

- **Without cause:** either party may terminate at any time **without cause on ninety (90) days' written notice** to the other.
- **For breach:** either party may terminate **immediately** if the other fails to **cure a material breach within thirty (30) days** after written notice of the breach.
- Either party may also terminate immediately if the other becomes insolvent, has a bankruptcy petition filed against it, or ceases doing business.

## Territory

- The base Agreement defined the Territory as **the United States**.
- The first Amendment (effective October 1, 1996) changed the Territory to **the United States and an additional territory that is redacted (`[*]`) in the public filing**.
- Amendment #2 (July 15, 1998) again amends Section 2, Territory, to "**United States, `[*]`**" — consistent with the first Amendment's change, but the additional territory's name is likewise redacted in both amendments. The current Territory cannot be stated more specifically than "the United States plus a redacted additional territory or territories" without an unredacted copy.

## Exclusivity

- **Original Territory (United States):** Distributor was the only distributor NETGEAR appointed in the Territory **only during the initial one-year term**, and that exclusivity was conditioned on Distributor carrying out the agreed marketing activities in the Marketing Plan (Exhibit 4). **Following the initial one-year term, for any extension or renewal term — which the Agreement has been in since 1997 — Distributor's appointment is non-exclusive**, and NETGEAR may appoint other distributors in the Territory at its sole discretion.
- **Added territory under the first Amendment:** Distributor was likewise the only distributor NETGEAR appointed in the redacted added territory, but again **only during the initial one-year period beginning on the Amendment Date** (October 1, 1996), subject to a corresponding marketing plan (Exhibit 4a); that exclusivity period has also since expired, and the appointment is non-exclusive for any extension or renewal term.
- **Carve-out:** even during an exclusive period, NETGEAR reserved the right to sell or license Products in the Territory to **customers other than distributors**, such as resellers that procure Products centrally for resale solely through their own (wholly or majority owned) retail outlets.

## Sales restrictions

Distributor may **not sell or license Products directly to end-use customers** without NETGEAR's express written consent; Distributor must sell through resale customers who in turn resell or transfer licenses to end users.

## Assignment

The Agreement **may not be assigned by either party without the other's prior written permission**, and that permission **may not be unreasonably withheld or delayed**.

## Governing law

The Agreement is governed by the **laws of the State of California**, excluding its conflict-of-laws rules.
