Before using the Brand in any manner, Licensee must **submit all proposed uses to Licensor for prior written approval**. Use must also be consistent with Licensor's quality and trademark-practice standards, and Licensee must affix any trademark notices Licensor or applicable law requires.

## 3. How the license can end

The term begins on the Effective Date and **continues in perpetuity**, subject only to termination under the following provisions — there is no fixed expiration date:

1. **Termination for convenience (Licensor only).** Licensor may terminate **immediately on written notice, for any reason**, including noncompliance with Licensor's standards and policies. Licensee has no corresponding right to terminate for convenience.
2. **Termination for uncured material breach.** Either party may terminate if the other materially breaches and does not cure within **15 days** of written notice (or an agreed extension). Licensor also has an immediate termination right if Licensee attempts a prohibited assignment or if a sublicensee's uncured material breach harms the Brand.
3. **Automatic termination tied to the advisory relationship.** The Agreement **terminates automatically, without notice**, if MS Capital Partners Adviser Inc. (or another Licensor affiliate) stops acting as Licensee's investment adviser, or if the investment adviser ceases to be a Licensor affiliate. Separately, Licensor may terminate on notice starting 30 days after Licensee tells Licensor that the Investment Advisory Agreement has ended or will not renew.

On termination for any reason, Licensee must immediately stop all use of the Permitted Activity, subject to applicable law, and the ownership, survival, liability and indemnification provisions continue to apply.

## 4. Assignment and corporate transactions

Licensee **may not assign, transfer, pledge, mortgage or otherwise encumber** this Agreement or its right to use the Brand, in whole or in part (including assuming it in bankruptcy), without Licensor's **prior written consent, given or withheld in Licensor's sole discretion**. The only carve-out is an assignment outside bankruptcy to a successor resulting solely from a name change.

Critically for the board, a **merger, change of control, reorganization, or sale of all or substantially all of Licensee's stock is deemed an "assignment"** requiring that same consent — regardless of whether Licensee survives the transaction or whether the deal would otherwise count as an assignment at law. The Agreement recites that Licensee's identity was a material condition inducing Licensor to sign. Any attempted assignment, merger or similar transaction without consent is **void ab initio** and **triggers immediate termination** of the Agreement. The board should treat Licensor consent as a prerequisite to any future merger, reorganization or sale of substantially all of Licensee's stock.

- **Indemnity runs one way, from Licensee to Licensor.** Licensee must defend, indemnify and hold harmless Licensor and its affiliates (and their directors, officers, employees, shareholders, investors, agents and representatives) against third-party claims arising from Licensee's breach of the Agreement, Licensee's operation of its business, or unauthorized use of the Brand that infringes a third party's rights.
